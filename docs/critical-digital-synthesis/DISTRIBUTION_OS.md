# Critical Digital Synthesis — Distribution OS v0.2

**Primary objective:** turn a small number of strong ideas into compounding discovery, trust, owned audience, paid opportunities and verified revenue.

## 0. Discovery-first entry rule

A strong idea is not automatically a discoverable idea.

Before asking a new audience to understand Critical Digital Synthesis, create a simple entry point that answers one concrete question, solves one problem, demonstrates one mechanism, or reveals one useful hidden distinction.

Default discovery formula:

`Useful Question + Demonstrable Example + One Original Concept + Evidence Link + One CTA`

The governing synthesis is documented in:
- `BEYOND_GOOGLE_SYNTHESIS.md`
- `DISCOVERY_DEPTH_ENGINE.json`
- `CONTENT_QUEUE_V01.md`

Core loop:

`DISCOVER → USE → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → FOLLOW → CONVERT → RETURN`

## 1. Completion gate

No work is considered complete until it has all four layers:

1. **Canonical home** — stable public source or archive.
2. **Discovery path** — at least one channel where new people can encounter it.
3. **Audience capture** — follow, newsletter, inquiry or other consent-based return path.
4. **Value path** — paid offer, editorial pitch, commission path, collaboration path, or an explicitly non-commercial credibility role.

If one layer is missing, the work remains `DISTRIBUTION_INCOMPLETE`.

## 2. Channel roles

### Canonical / permanent
- Public GitHub source and manifest
- Future dedicated domain
- DOI/archive layer for research when legitimately available

### Discovery
- Searchable long-form video
- Short clips derived from canonical work
- Paid editorial pitches
- Visual case studies
- Professional/research networks where account eligibility permits

### Audience capture
- Newsletter / updates form
- Research inquiry form
- Project inquiry form

### Monetization
- Paid editorial commissions
- AI legitimacy & transparency review
- Research / talks / workshops
- Selected creative and digital commissions

## 3. Content routing

### Essay / argument
`idea → full draft → rights check → paid pitch → public preview only → discovery asset → publish/archive after rights decision → atomize`

### Research artifact
`question → preregistration → raw data → analysis → public method/ledger → discovery explanation → archive/DOI when eligible → talk/service bridge`

### Art / visual project
`work → curated case study → searchable visual/video presentation → inquiry path → licensing/commission/collaboration`

### Tool / experiment
`working prototype → public explanation → reproducible evidence → demo → service/research bridge`

## 4. Discovery–Depth rule

Discovery gets attention; depth earns memory.

A discovery asset should not require the audience to know CDS terminology. Once the viewer understands the practical issue, introduce **one** distinctive concept such as:
- Normative Compression
- Compressed Power
- Source-of-Constraint Transparency
- Utility Is Not Authorization
- Digital Iceberg

Do not front-load several abstract concepts into the first contact.

## 5. Comment-to-demand loop

Substantive audience responses are classified as:
1. question,
2. alternative,
3. confusion,
4. objection,
5. use case,
6. retention signal,
7. qualified inquiry.

Use this as editorial research. Do not turn every comment into content; verify demand and strategic relevance first.

## 6. Atomization rule

One strong canonical work should generate multiple discovery assets without changing the underlying claim:

- 1 canonical article / research artifact / visual project
- 1 searchable video
- 2–4 short clips
- 1 visual concept card
- 1 concise professional/post version
- 1 newsletter/update item
- 1 clear CTA

Do not create ten new ideas when one strong idea has not yet completed its distribution cycle.

## 7. Anti-slop requirement

AI-assisted production must contain at least one non-generic value element:
- original test/result,
- original framework,
- original visual analysis,
- direct source comparison,
- personal/creative work,
- documented experiment,
- evidence-grounded new synthesis.

Utility is necessary for discovery; distinctiveness is necessary for durable identity.

## 8. Metric truth hierarchy

### Attention
- impressions
- views
- profile visits

### Retention
- follows
- subscribers
- returning visitors

### Intent
- substantive replies
- qualified inquiries
- editor interest
- meeting requests

### Commercial evidence
- agreed paid commission
- contractually valid paid engagement
- verified payment

**Views, followers, clicks, drafts, pitches and inquiries are not revenue. Only verified payment counts as revenue.**

## 9. Weekly operating rule

Prioritize, in order:

1. distribute existing strong work,
2. create/optimize discovery entry points for it,
3. convert attention into audience capture,
4. convert qualified interest into bounded offers,
5. improve canonical assets,
6. only then create new major projects.

Target ratio: **at least as much distribution work as creation work** until a repeatable discovery channel exists.

## 10. Rights protection

- Keep full manuscripts unpublished while first-publication rights are being pitched.
- Publish teasers/excerpts only when compatible with target outlet rules.
- Confirm fee, copyright, license and exclusivity before sending a full manuscript when terms are unclear.
- Do not treat platform visibility as permission to give away broad rights.

## 11. Eligibility boundary

Do not bypass age, identity, contracting, payment, tax or platform eligibility requirements. Paid work proceeds only through lawful, eligible routes. Use institutional or legally appropriate adult-supported contracting where required.

## 12. Default decision rule

Before creating anything new, answer:

- Who is this for?
- What useful question gets them through the door?
- What is its canonical home?
- Where will the right audience discover it?
- What one CDS concept makes it memorable?
- What evidence lets them verify it?
- How can interested people return?
- What is the legitimate value path?
- What evidence would show the distribution worked?

If those answers are missing, solve distribution before expanding production.
