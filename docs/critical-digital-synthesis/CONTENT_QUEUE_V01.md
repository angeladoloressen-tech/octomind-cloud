## First production sequence

Do **not** produce all titles at once.

Start with a three-asset validation sprint:

1. **Why Does ChatGPT Say “I Can’t”?**
2. **5 AI Limitations That Are Not Laws**
3. **Can an AI Tell You Who Made the Rule?**

For each:
- 1 searchable 4–8 minute video
- 2 short clips
- 1 concept card
- 1 canonical source/evidence page
- 1 primary CTA

Measure:
- click/view signal,
- completion/retention where available,
- comments/questions,
- follows/subscriptions,
- newsletter signups,
- qualified inquiries.

Do not count any of these as revenue unless payment is actually verified.

## Comment-to-content rule

Every substantive comment is classified once:
- question,
- alternative,
- confusion,
- objection,
- use case,
- retention signal,
- qualified inquiry.

Only create a follow-up when there is evidence of demand or strategic relevance. Avoid content-volume inflation.

## Language strategy

Default production can use one master script and produce accessible language versions/subtitles when platform and workflow support it. Do not claim native cultural equivalence from machine translation alone; review terminology for research concepts.

Priority language logic for CDS:
- English: widest research/editorial discovery
- German: professional/institutional reach in Germany
- Turkish: distinctive cultural/educational audience and original voice

The canonical claim/evidence source remains consistent across languages.
