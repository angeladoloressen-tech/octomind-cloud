# Who Authorized the Machine? — Reader Path

## Useful is not the same as authorized

Conversational AI can be helpful, fast and persuasive while still leaving a basic question unanswered: **who authorized the system to exercise a particular kind of power?**

The Critical Digital Synthesis essay *Who Authorized the Machine?* examines the legitimacy gap that appears when a conversational interface influences what people can see, ask, learn or do while the source of authority remains difficult to identify.
