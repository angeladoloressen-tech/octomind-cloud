# AI Transparency Self-Audit Kit — EN/DE

**30 questions to test whether an AI system clearly communicates its rules, limits, authority and user recourse.**

The AI Transparency Self-Audit Kit is a practical bilingual English/German workbook from Critical Digital Synthesis. It turns the project’s research on source-of-constraint transparency, contestability and normative compression into a repeatable self-audit.

## What is inside

- 9-page bilingual PDF workbook
- 30 scored audit questions
- 6 review categories
- summary scorecard
- editable CSV worksheet
- internal-use license

## What it reviews

1. Constraint source
2. Authority and rule ownership
3. User recourse and contestability
4. Data, access and permissions
5. Communication quality
6. Governance and accountability

## Who it is for

AI product teams, researchers, educators, consultants, governance teams and organizations using conversational AI.
