# Normative Compression — A Practical Entry Point

## When an AI says “I can’t,” what kind of limit is it?

A conversational AI refusal can hide very different sources behind the same first-person sentence. A useful first step is to separate at least five categories:

1. technical limitation
2. permission or access limitation
3. provider policy
4. law or external regulation
5. uncertainty or insufficient knowledge

Critical Digital Synthesis uses **normative compression** as a research term for cases where institutionally different constraints are compressed into a single conversational surface and their source becomes harder for the user to identify or contest.

## Try the question

When a system refuses a request, ask:

> What kind of limitation is this, where does it come from, and what can the user do next?

A strong answer should distinguish the source of the constraint instead of presenting every boundary as an unexplained property of “the AI.”
